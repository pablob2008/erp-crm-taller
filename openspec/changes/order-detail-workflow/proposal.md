# Proposal: order-detail-workflow

## 1. Problem Statement
The work order details view lacks global management actions (edit order, delete order, share summary to WhatsApp, direct WhatsApp chat link, print trigger placeholder), complete 360-degree information (client phone/email, full device specs, intake diagnostic/suggested solution), and an integrated financial settlement & delivery workflow (random expense logging on order, order delivery on credit vs collect & deliver with mandatory payment method and note syncing).

## 2. Proposed Solution
Create an integrated order detail workflow that provides:
1. Global Management Actions: Edit, Delete, WhatsApp summary sharing, WhatsApp direct chat, and print triggers.
2. Complete 360-Degree Information: Display full client contact details (phone, email), comprehensive device specifications, intake diagnostic, and suggested solutions.
3. Financial Settlement & Delivery Workflow: Support for random expense logging on orders, distinguishing between order delivery on credit vs. collect & deliver, and enforcing mandatory payment methods and note syncing.
