## Why

The work order details view lacks global management actions (edit order, delete order, share summary to WhatsApp, direct WhatsApp chat link, print trigger placeholder), complete 360-degree information (client phone/email, full device specs, intake diagnostic/suggested solution), and an integrated financial settlement & delivery workflow (random expense logging on order, order delivery on credit vs collect & deliver with mandatory payment method and note syncing).

## What Changes

- Add global management actions: edit order, delete order, share summary to WhatsApp, direct WhatsApp chat link, print trigger placeholder.
- Add complete 360-degree information: client phone/email, full device specs, intake diagnostic/suggested solution.
- Integrate financial settlement & delivery workflow: random expense logging on order, order delivery on credit vs collect & deliver with mandatory payment method and note syncing.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `work-order-lifecycle`: Defines the requirements for work order management actions, complete detailed information views, and integrated financial settlement & delivery workflows.

### Modified Capabilities
- (None)

## Impact

Work order details view, financial settlement components, delivery workflow, and related UI/backend modules.
