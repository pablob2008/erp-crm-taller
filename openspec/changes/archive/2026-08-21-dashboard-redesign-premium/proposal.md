## Why

The current dashboard lacks actionable metrics and modern visual hierarchy. This revamp brings a Neumorphic premium look (30% high-impact widgets, 70% functional canvas) feeding directly from live database metrics.

## What Changes

- Hero KPIs (Active Workshop, Ready for Pickup, Today's Cash, Stock Alerts).
- Pipeline visualization for work order states.
- Live recent orders table with customer and WhatsApp quick links.
- Recent activity feed for cash movements.
- Neumorphic quick action buttons.
